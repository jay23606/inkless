Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into as of the date of the final signature.

1. Purpose

The parties wish to explore a possible business relationship concerning a software product (the “Purpose”). In doing so, either party may share information that should remain confidential.

2. Confidential information

“Confidential Information” means non-public business, technical, financial, or product information disclosed for the Purpose, whether communicated verbally, visually, electronically, or in writing.

3. Responsibilities

Each receiving party will use reasonable care to protect Confidential Information, use it only for the Purpose, and share it only with people who need it and are bound by similar duties.

4. Term

These confidentiality obligations continue for two years from each disclosure.
